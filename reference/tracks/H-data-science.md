# Track H: Data Science and Classical ML

**You become:** a data scientist who answers real questions with data and statistics, and also works with ML models.  
**Requires:** Levels 0, 2, 3. **Time:** 200-350 h. Good for jobs that want DS/analytics/ML engineer profiles.

## Learning goals

- SQL and data wrangling at scale; data modeling; pandas/Polars; visualization.
- Statistics for decisions: inference, hypothesis testing, regression, A/B testing and experimentation, causal inference basics, Bayesian methods.
- Tabular ML: feature engineering, gradient boosting, time series, forecasting, recommender basics.
- Communication: dashboards, written analysis, stakeholder framing.
- Data engineering light: Spark/dbt/Airflow basics, warehouses.

## Projects

1. **Experimentation analysis**: simulate and analyze an A/B test with power analysis, sequential testing pitfalls, and a clear recommendation memo.
2. **Forecasting project**: time-series forecasting with proper backtesting; compare statistical and ML baselines.
3. **Causal question**: observational dataset, DAG, adjustment, sensitivity analysis.
4. **Dashboard and memo**: a stakeholder-facing analysis with an executive summary.
5. **Kaggle or public-data competition** with a well-documented write-up.

## Done when

You can take an ambiguous business question, define the metric, get and clean the data, analyze it with sound statistics, and communicate a decision with honest uncertainty.
